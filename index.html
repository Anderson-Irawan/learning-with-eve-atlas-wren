03 / MAKE UNCERTAINTY TESTABLEProposed behavior ✳

A useful “I don't know”

Three reasons
to pause.

These are proposed abstention triggers. Whether Alicorn follows them reliably is unknown.

  1. 01

    No relevant note

    The retrieved material does not answer the question.

  2. 02

    Conflicting notes

    Sources disagree and the conflict cannot be resolved.

  3. 03

    Unsupported claim

    A key detail would have to be inferred rather than cited.

CONFIDENCE WORDS ALONE ARE NOT PROOF04 / 07
04 / MEASURE THE RIGHT THINGSA proposed scorecard ✳

Don't collapse quality into one number

Four lenses on
one answer.

01

Correctness

Is the answer right?

02

Evidence support

Does each claim follow from its source?

03

Correct abstention

Does it hold back when evidence is missing?

04

Useful coverage

Does it answer what the evidence can support?

Compare with the same hand-checked questions and fixed settings; review errors question by question. These are proposed measures, not measured scores.

Source: Day 2 Research — Fair Evaluation; Day 2 Findings — Plain-Language Summary
